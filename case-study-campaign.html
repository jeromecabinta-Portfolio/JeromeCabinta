Social Media Content Campaign

A complete, execution-ready 7-day social media content campaign designed for a multi-campus community organization. Demonstrating strategic content pillars, Canva visual creatives, an end-to-end scheduling workflow, and a verified measurement framework without invented statistics.

Hypothetical Client Agape Community Fellowship
Primary Goal Increase Service Attendance & Engagement
Role & Deliverables Social Media VA & Content Designer
Tools Used Canva, Meta Business Suite, Notion, Photoshop
01 / Foundation

Client Brief, Audience & Content Strategy

Establishing clear objectives, audience personas, and content pillars before designing a single pixel.

The Business Goal & Challenge

The Problem: The organization relied on last-minute, text-heavy announcements posted irregularly across Instagram and Facebook. As a result, organic reach was declining, young adults felt disconnected from midweek events, and event volunteer onboarding had stalled.

The Goal: Build a predictable, high-retention weekly content rhythm that transforms followers from passive scrollers into active attendees. The objective was to increase weekend service attendance, generate save-worthy midweek devotionals, and streamline volunteer sign-ups through clear, low-friction calls to action.

VA Execution Scope: Manage the end-to-end weekly cycle: copy drafting, Canva template design, client approval staging in Notion, automated scheduling via Meta Business Suite, and first-30-minute comment moderation.

Audience & Core Content Pillars

Target Audience: Young adults, university students, and young families (ages 18–38) seeking authentic community, spiritual encouragement, and opportunities to connect locally.

P1

Inspiration & Reflection (40%)

High-share scripture quote cards, Monday motivation, and reflection carousels optimized for saves and story reposts.

P2

Community & Event Invites (35%)

Weekend service reminders, youth night fellowships, and acoustic worship nights with direct RSVP link CTAs.

P3

People & Behind-the-Scenes (25%)

Volunteer spotlights, community outreach recaps, and interactive question polls that spark genuine conversations.

05 / Accountable Analytics

Proposed Framework to Measure Success

Note: Because this is a strategic concept project, no fabricated engagement metrics or fictional results are presented. Below is the validated KPI framework used to measure real client campaign performance.

True Engagement Rate (%)

Measures genuine audience resonance against total accounts reached, rather than vanity impressions.

(Likes + Comments + Saves + Shares) / Reach × 100

High-Intent Save & Share Ratio

Tracks how many users save quote carousels or share announcements to personal stories, signaling genuine utility.

Benchmark: >15-20 Saves & Shares per 1k Reach

UTM Link-in-Bio Clicks

Tracks direct conversions from social traffic to event registrations, service sign-ups, and full podcast views.

Google Analytics 4 + UTM Tag Tracking
