Anatomy of an Engaging Post Caption

Every caption is systematically written to halt passive scrolling, communicate value, and trigger community action:

1. The Hook (Line 1)
"Finding peace when your schedule is at maximum capacity isn't about doing more — it's about what you choose to let go of."
2. The Core Value (Body)
"Swipe through today's carousel for 5 micro-habits you can implement in under 10 minutes to protect your energy and renew focus."
3. Community Discussion Prompt
"Which of these 5 practices resonates most with your routine right now? Let us know below 👇"
4. Clear Next-Step Action (CTA)
"Tap the link in bio for the complete sermon audio and discussion guide."
5. Curated Community Hashtags
#CommunityFellowship #FaithAndWork #DailyHabits #ChurchOnline #MindfulLiving
