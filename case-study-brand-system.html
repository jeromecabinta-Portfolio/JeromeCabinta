Apex Outdoors: Brand Design System

A comprehensive visual identity rollout and multi-format social media design system engineered for a sustainable technical outdoor apparel brand. Demonstrating color tokens, typography rules, cross-platform graphics, and strategic brief alignment.

Concept Client Apex Outdoors • DTC Technical Outerwear
Design Problem Solved Cross-Platform Fragmentation & Production Speed
Key Deliverables Brand Tokens, Grid Rules, 16 Multi-Format Assets
Software Tools Figma, Adobe Photoshop, Canva, Illustrator
01 / Strategic Context

Brand Brief & Target Audience Profile

Understanding the commercial landscape, consumer mindset, and identity hurdles before establishing visual rules.

The Brand Brief

Background: Apex Outdoors engineered high-performance, weather-resistant jackets and hiking backpacks using 100% recycled technical fibers. However, their digital touchpoints suffered from severe visual fragmentation: product posts looked like generic catalog cutouts, fonts varied across platforms, and creating a single social campaign took over 90 minutes of manual layout work.

The Objective: Develop an authoritative, modular visual identity and social media design system that transitions Apex Outdoors into a premium direct-to-consumer (DTC) lifestyle brand. The system needed to feel rugged enough for high-altitude peak expeditions yet polished enough for modern urban commuters, with templated assets a Social Media VA can spin up in under 15 minutes.

Core Brand Mantra: "Rooted in Peaks, Made for You. Good for you. Good for the wild."

Target Audience Profile

Demographics: Men and women aged 22–45, outdoor enthusiasts, trail runners, weekend backpackers, and eco-conscious remote professionals residing in North America and Europe.

Psychographics & Values:

  • Durability Over Trends: Strongly prefer "buy once, wear for years" philosophy over fast fashion.
  • Radical Transparency: Expect clear technical specs (waterproof ratings, fabric denier, recycled textile percentages).
  • Understated Outdoor Aesthetic: Drawn to organic earthy neutrals rather than neon sports gimmicks.
02 / Design System Tokens

Color Palette, Typography & Visual Rules

Strict design tokens engineered to guarantee unmistakable brand recognition across fast-scrolling mobile feeds.

Systematic Color Tokens

The palette draws direct inspiration from high-altitude alpine terrain: pine canopies, mountain granite, unbleached canvas, and morning ridge light. Built on a strict 60-30-10 distribution rule to prevent visual clutter.

Alpine Olive #3B4632 Dominant Anchor (60%)
Summit Cream #EAE6DC Soft Background (30%)
Ice White #FAF7F2 High Contrast Text
Obsidian #262626 Deep Structure & Copy
Trail Gold #C5A059 CTA & Badges (10%)

Typography Hierarchy

A tailored tripartite font pairing combining timeless heritage with modern mobile legibility and technical engineering data.

Display & Hero Headings
Rooted in Peaks.

Serif Display (Georgia / Playfair) • Editorial prestige, heritage authority, and cinematic gravitas.

Body & Subheadings
Engineered for extreme mountain weather.

Modern Sans (Plus Jakarta Sans / Inter) • Ultra-legible at small sizes on bright mobile screens.

Technical Badges & Coordinates
LAT 46.8523° N • GSM 240 • DWR 20K

Monospace (Space Mono) • Renders trail coordinates, fabric ratings, and SKU specs like gear blueprints.

8px Baseline Grid

All canvas margins, paddings, and card gaps adhere to strict 8px / 16px / 24px multiples, ensuring mathematical alignment across Canva and Figma.

108px Safe Zones

Vertical 9:16 stories and reels maintain 108px top/bottom exclusion margins so platform usernames and caption bars never obscure primary copy.

Authentic Photo Grading

Natural morning or overcast mountain lighting with -10% desaturation and subtle analog film grain. Never over-saturated or artificial studio backdrops.

Logo Clearspace

The mountain peak icon requires an exclusion zone equal to 100% of the peak icon height on all sides before adjacent text or borders.

04 / Problem-Solving Impact

How This System Solves the Client Brief

A breakdown of the practical, commercial advantages achieved by establishing this design system.

1. Eliminates Visual Fragmentation

By locking in strict token rules (Alpine Olive + Summit Cream + Serif Header), every post across Instagram, Facebook, and Web looks unmistakably like Apex Outdoors. Followers instantly recognize the brand before even reading the handle.

2. Cuts Asset Creation from 90m to 15m

Standardizing 1:1, 4:5, and 9:16 templates in Canva and Figma allows a solo Social Media VA or marketing coordinator to drop in new product photography and text in under 15 minutes, drastically lowering overhead costs.

3. Maximizes Outdoor Mobile Readability

By pairing high-contrast earthy dark tones with clean geometric sans-serif body typography, graphics remain 100% legible on mobile screens even in direct sunlight on hiking trails.

4. Balances Rugged Utility with Luxury

The serif typography and refined monochromatic layouts elevate the brand above generic sportswear, commanding premium DTC price points while honoring authentic trail performance.
